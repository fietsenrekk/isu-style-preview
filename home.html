UCHI is two chairs on Klapdorp, and two hairstylists: Labi and Donovan.

The room stays open late on purpose. Monday to Friday the door opens at 10:00 and does not close until 22:00, with an hour's rest in the middle of the day. Weekends the chairs are empty.

Cuts, colour, balayage and blow-dry. One price list for everyone, following the length of your hair and the work it takes. The full list sits on the reservation page, next to the button that books it.

Book the chair and the time is yours.
