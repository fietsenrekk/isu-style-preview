KIRU is one chair on Klapdorp. The door opens when most of the city has already closed for the night.

There is no men's price here and no women's price. What you pay follows the length of your hair and the work it takes: clippers, clippers and scissors, or scissors only. Three prices, and the same three for everyone.

Alabi puts it more plainly than we can. “I don't do genders, I do hair … Same prices for all.”

Wednesday, Thursday and Friday the door opens at 20:00 and stays open until 22:00. Sunday and Monday, all day. Book the chair and the time is yours.
